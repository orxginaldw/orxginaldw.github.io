Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Binwoken (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes the information we collect when you use binwoken.sh and related services.

Information we collect

When you log in or buy premium, we may collect information needed to run the site and deliver what you paid for, including:

  • Your Discord user ID, username, and avatar, from Discord login
  • Order and billing identifiers from our payment providers
  • The IP address used with some features, for security and abuse prevention
  • Optional Discord webhook URLs and Roblox user IDs you add to a watch list
  • API keys you generate on the site
  • Basic technical information such as browser details and server logs

Payments are processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Some features use Google reCAPTCHA to reduce abuse.

How we use it

We use collected information to:

  • Process purchases and deliver software access / keys
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the website and services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

Your information may be processed by services we use to operate the site and take payments, including Discord, PayPal, Cloudflare, and Google. Those providers handle information under their own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including where retention is required by law.
